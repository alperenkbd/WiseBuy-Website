Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 19, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to WiseBuy ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how we collect, use, and safeguard your information. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection and usage in the WiseBuy: AI Shopping Manager mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account using Apple Sign-In, we collect:

  • Your Apple ID (anonymized user identifier)
  • Email address (if you choose to share it)
  • Display name (if you choose to share it)

2.2 Shopping Data

To provide AI-powered shopping recommendations, we collect:

  • Product information you input (name, price, description)
  • Product photos you upload or capture
  • Your answers to decision-making questions
  • AI analysis results and recommendations
  • Your shopping history and decisions (BUY, WAIT, GIFT, SKIP)

2.3 Usage Data

  • App usage statistics (features used, frequency)
  • Environmental impact metrics (CO₂ saved, money saved)
  • Subscription status and purchase history
  • Device information (OS version, app version)

2.4 Analytics and Advertising

For free tier users, we use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • IP address and general location
  • Ad interaction data

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide AI Analysis: Process your shopping queries using Google Gemini 2.5 AI to generate personalized recommendations
  • Sync Across Devices: Store your data in Firebase Firestore to enable cross-device synchronization
  • Manage Subscriptions: Process and manage your premium subscription through RevenueCat
  • Track Environmental Impact: Calculate and display your positive environmental contributions
  • Display Ads: Show relevant advertisements to free tier users
  • Improve the App: Analyze usage patterns to enhance features and user experience
  • Provide Support: Respond to your inquiries and technical issues

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard practices:

  • Firebase Firestore: All user data is stored in Google's secure cloud infrastructure with encryption at rest and in transit
  • Firebase Authentication: User authentication is handled securely through Firebase Auth with Apple Sign-In
  • Local Storage: Some data is cached locally on your device for offline access
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for their marketing purposes

5. Third-Party Services

WiseBuy integrates with the following third-party services:

5.1 Google Gemini 2.5 AI

Product information and your responses are sent to Google's Gemini API for AI analysis. Google's privacy policy applies to this processing.

5.2 Firebase (Google)

We use Firebase for authentication and data storage. Google's Firebase privacy policy applies.

5.3 RevenueCat

Subscription management and in-app purchases are handled through RevenueCat. RevenueCat's privacy policy applies to subscription data.

5.4 Google AdMob

Free tier users see ads powered by Google AdMob. AdMob's privacy policy and Google's advertising policies apply.

6. Data Retention

  • Active Accounts: Your data is retained as long as your account is active
  • Account Deletion: You can request account deletion by contacting us. We will delete your data within 30 days
  • Subscription Data: Purchase history may be retained for legal and accounting purposes

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Export: Request an export of your shopping history
  • Opt-Out: Disable personalized ads through your device settings

To exercise these rights, contact us at alperenkabadayi0110@gmail.com

8. Children's Privacy

WiseBuy is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

9. International Users

WiseBuy is available globally with support for multiple currencies (USD, EUR, GBP, TRY) and languages (English, Turkish). Your data may be processed and stored in servers located in different countries. By using WiseBuy, you consent to the transfer of your data to these locations.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by:

  • Posting the new policy on this page
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Sending an in-app notification for material changes

Your continued use of WiseBuy after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Environmental Commitment

As part of our mission, 15% of all premium subscription revenue is donated to environmental causes. While we track aggregate donation amounts, individual user contribution data is not shared publicly.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), we process your data based on:

  • Contract: Processing is necessary to provide the services you requested
